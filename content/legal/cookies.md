A cookie is a small piece of text that a website asks your browser to keep, and to send back on your next visit. Howdy uses only **two** cookies, and both exist only to make Howdy itself work the way you asked. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and no cookies from anyone else.

## The cookies we use

### Your session (essential)

- **Name:** `__Host-howdy_session`
- **What it does:** keeps you signed in. It holds a long random code that proves to Howdy that this browser is signed in to your account. The code is useless on its own, and we store only a scrambled version of it.
- **How it is protected:** it can only be sent over an encrypted connection and only to Howdy, and the page's own code cannot read it. That makes it much harder to steal.
- **How long it lasts:** until you sign out. A session also ends after 14 days without use, and after 60 days at most.
- **Can you turn it off?** Not if you want to sign in. Without it, Howdy cannot know it is you.

### Your theme (preference)

- **Name:** `howdy_theme`
- **What it does:** remembers whether you chose the light (Daylight) or dark (Dusk) theme, so that the page opens in the right colours. It holds only the word `daylight` or `dusk`.
- **When it is set:** only if you pick a theme yourself. If you follow your device's setting, there is no theme cookie.
- **How long it lasts:** one year, or until you choose "follow my device" again.

## Analytics and advertising

Howdy uses **no** analytics cookies and **no** advertising or marketing cookies, and it does not load anyone else's tracking code.

If that ever changes, we will update this policy first. For anything that is not strictly needed to run Howdy, we will ask for your permission before setting it, and give you a way to say no.

## Other ways we remember things

Howdy does not use your browser's local storage or similar tools to track you. While you are using the site, your browser may keep pages you just visited in memory for up to 30 seconds, so that switching tabs is quick. That memory is cleared when you close the tab.

## Controlling cookies

Every browser lets you see and delete cookies, and block them for a site. Look for "Cookies" or "Site data" in your browser's privacy settings. If you delete Howdy's cookies you will be signed out, and your theme choice will be forgotten. If you block them, you will not be able to sign in.
