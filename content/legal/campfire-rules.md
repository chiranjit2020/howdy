Howdy is a campfire, not a stadium. It is small circles of people who chose each other, sitting close enough to hear one another. These rules keep it warm. They apply everywhere on Howdy: your Porch, Fences, Post Cards, Whispers, Tributes, Signals, Town Halls, your call sign and your photo.

## Respect the campfire

Be human. Talk to people the way you would if they were sitting right across the fire from you. Disagree with ideas, not with the person. Kindness is not weakness here, it is the whole point.

## No harassment

No intimidation, threats, stalking or bullying. Do not pile on, and do not keep contacting someone who has asked you to stop, or who has blocked or muted you. Unwanted sexual comments and advances count as harassment.

## No hate

Do not attack or demean people for who they are: their religion, caste, race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, sexual orientation, disability or illness. Slurs and dehumanising jokes are not welcome anywhere near the fire.

## No doxxing

Never share someone's private information without their permission. That means home addresses, phone numbers, documents, private photos, screenshots of private Whispers, or anything that helps someone find a person who does not want to be found.

## No spam

No automated accounts, mass Post Cards, mass Yos, copy-paste campaigns, scams or misleading links. Do not ask for, or offer, Pals, Tributes or Marks in bulk. The Vibe Matrix is for real vibes.

## No impersonation

Be yourself. Do not pretend to be another person, a brand, or the Howdy team. Parody and fan accounts must say so clearly, in their name.

## Respect boundaries

Blocks, mutes and privacy settings matter. Do not try to get around them with another account, and do not ask someone else to pass messages on. If someone's Porch is for Pals only, it is for Pals only.

## Keep it safe and legal

Nothing illegal. Nothing that sexualises minors, ever: we report it to the authorities. No violent threats, no encouraging self-harm, no graphic violence meant to shock, no selling drugs or weapons. Only post what you have the right to share.

## Look after each other

If something feels wrong, you do not have to put up with it. **Mute** people you would rather not hear from, **Restrict** someone so that what they write waits for you, **Block** anyone you want gone, and **Report** anything that breaks these rules (from their Porch, or from the card's menu). Reports are private: the person is never told who reported them.

If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first. In India, call **112**.

## When rules are broken

A person reviews every report. Depending on what happened, we may remove the content, limit what an account can do, or suspend it, and serious or repeated breaks can mean losing the account for good. If you think we got it wrong, write to [{{contactEmail}}](mailto:{{contactEmail}}) and someone will look again. The [Terms](/terms) explain this in more detail.

These rules will grow as the campfire does. Thank you for keeping it warm.
